[performance · low] Two minor backoff inefficiencies in match_with_retry: (1) `await
asyncio.sleep(2.0)` runs inside `async with sem`, so a failing task holds one of the concurrency
slots while idling — during provider-failure bursts the effective parallelism collapses to near zero
even though no requests are in flight; (2) the sleep also executes after the 3rd and final failed
attempt before falling through to `return m`, which is pure wasted latency. Acquire the semaphore
only around the actual call and skip the sleep after the last attempt. Correctness is unaffected;
only total wall time suffers.
