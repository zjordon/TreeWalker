# Alphabet Q2 2025 Earnings — 3 Interesting Data Points

Source: Alphabet Inc. Q2 2025 Earnings Release (July 23, 2025)
Document: 2025q2-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf (displayed in browser; live URL: https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2025/q2/2025q2-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf — the original abc.xyz asset URL now redirects away)

1. Consolidated revenues: Q2 2025 revenues grew 14% year-over-year (13% in constant currency) to $96.4 billion, with double-digit growth across Google Search & other, YouTube ads, Google subscriptions/platforms/devices, and Google Cloud.

2. Google Cloud: revenues increased 32% year-over-year to $13.6 billion, led by growth in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) across core GCP products, AI Infrastructure, and Generative AI Solutions.

3. Profitability: net income increased 19% and EPS increased 22% to $2.31; total operating income grew 14% with an operating margin of 32.4% (helped by revenue growth and expense efficiencies, partly offset by a charge for a legal settlement in principle).
